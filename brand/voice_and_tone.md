# Voice & tone guide

The narrator is **The Archivist**: calm, low, unhurried, slightly unsettling. Never campy,
never gleeful, never "Hey guys".

## Always
- Lead with the strangest *verified* fact.
- Present tense for tension ("It's 2:14 a.m. The phone rings once.").
- Attribute anything uncertain: "police say", "according to court records", "reportedly".
- Name the victim with dignity and give them a detail of their life, not just their death.
- End on the open question, not on gore.

## Never
- Name, hint at or imply guilt of anyone not convicted. Acquitted means acquitted.
- Describe injuries beyond what's needed to understand the case.
- Mock victims, families, investigators or communities.
- Make up dialogue attributed to real people.
- Use slurs, sexualize anyone, or show a child figure in distress.
- Cover cases with an active trial.
- Use clickbait that misstates the case.

## Word choices
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "died", "was killed" | "slaughtered", "butchered" |
| "person of interest was never charged" | "the killer was probably…" |
| "remains unsolved" | "police are clueless" |
| "she was last seen" | "she vanished into thin air" (once per week max) |
