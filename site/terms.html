The content

Cold Save videos tell real cases for informational and entertainment purposes. They are researched from public records, official case databases and published reporting, and the sources are listed in each video's description. They are not legal advice and make no accusation against anyone who was not convicted. Visuals and narration are AI-assisted and stylized; they are not real footage.

Corrections and removals

If something in a video is inaccurate, or you are connected to a case and want a video changed or removed, contact us and we will review it promptly. Information about a case should go to the investigating agency named in the video or to NamUs (namus.nij.ojp.gov).
