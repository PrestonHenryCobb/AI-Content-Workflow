Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers the Cold Save YouTube channel (@coldsavecases) and "Cold Save Pipeline", the private tool that publishes the channel's videos ("we", "the app").

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By watching or interacting with Cold Save on YouTube you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the app

Only the channel owner, signed in to the channel owner's own Google account. There is no public sign-in, and the app never asks viewers to connect their accounts.

What data the app accesses, and why

  • The channel's own account and videos (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube, youtube.force-ssl): to upload and schedule the channel's videos, set channel branding and playlists, add videos to playlists, and post the channel's own comment on its videos.
  • Public comments on the channel's own videos (text only, top-level comments): read once a week to count which real cases viewers are requesting. Commenters' names, usernames, profile images and IDs are not stored; only extracted case names and how often they were requested are kept.
  • The channel's own analytics (scope: yt-analytics.readonly): per-video aggregate statistics (views, average percentage viewed, average view duration, likes, comment count, subscribers gained) to learn which formats perform best. These are channel-level aggregates, not information about individual viewers.

What is stored, and where

  • The OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never published.
  • Aggregate video statistics, scheduling records and extracted case-name counts are stored in the pipeline's state database in the project's GitHub repository.
  • No cookies, trackers or analytics scripts are used on this website.

Sharing

We do not sell or share YouTube user data. Comment text is processed by Anthropic's Claude model solely to extract requested case names, and is not retained afterwards. Images are generated by fal.ai from text prompts that contain no YouTube user data. Nothing else is sent to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data obtained from YouTube API Services is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as the YouTube API Services policies require: per-video statistics older than 30 days are deleted automatically, keeping only the app's own derived win/loss counts per video format. Comment text is never stored. If the app is retired, its stored data is deleted. To have anything related to you removed, contact us (below).

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings: third-party access.

Children

Cold Save's videos are not made for children, and the app does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.
